# Refund Policy

**Effective Date**: 2025  
**Last Updated**: 2025

joymagic is committed to providing a fair, transparent, and reliable refund process for all users. Because our product consists of digital goods and cloud-based compute services delivered instantly upon purchase, our refund rules differ from those for physical products. This policy describes when refunds may be granted, how they are processed, and the obligations of both users and joymagic.

---

## 1. Eligibility for Refunds

joymagic provides refunds in the following situations:

### 1.1 Duplicate Purchases

If you accidentally make the same purchase more than once, we will refund the duplicated transaction(s) upon verification.

### 1.2 Technical Issues Preventing Use

If you are unable to use joymagic due to a verified technical error on our platform, and the issue cannot be resolved by our support team, you may request a refund for unused credits.

### 1.3 Misdelivery of Credits

If purchased credits were not delivered to your account due to a system error, and we are unable to restore the credits within a reasonable timeframe, you are eligible for a refund.

---

## 2. Situations Not Eligible for Refunds

Refunds **cannot** be issued in the following cases:

### 2.1 Used Credits

Credits consumed to generate images or use the service are non-refundable, as compute resources have already been provided.

### 2.2 Change of Mind

We cannot offer refunds because a user:

* No longer wants the product
* Intended to use fewer credits
* Prefers a different service or model
* Misunderstood features that are already documented

### 2.3 Violations of Our Policies

Users who violate our Terms of Service or Content Moderation Policy (e.g., attempts to generate harmful or prohibited content) are not eligible for refunds.

---

## 3. Response Time

In accordance with Creem's requirements:

### 3.1 Merchant Response Window

joymagic responds to all refund requests **within 3 business days**.

### 3.2 Failure to Respond

If we do not respond within this timeframe, **Creem may issue a refund on our behalf** to ensure customer satisfaction.

---

## 4. Refund Review Process

To process a refund request, we may require:

* Transaction ID or receipt
* Email used for purchase
* Screenshots or error logs (if applicable)
* A brief description of the issue

Once your request is reviewed:

* Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method
* Refund processing times vary depending on the payment provider (typically 3–10 business days)

---

## 5. One-Time Purchases vs. Subscriptions

joymagic currently uses a **credit-based system**. However, this section also applies to any future subscription plans.

### 5.1 Credits (One-Time Purchases)

Refunds apply only to **unused** credits.

### 5.2 Subscriptions (If Introduced Later)

Subscription refunds follow these principles:

* Subscriptions can be canceled at any time
* Previously used subscription cycles cannot be refunded
* Renewals that were unintentionally charged may be refunded if users contact support within 48 hours

---

## 6. Chargebacks

If a user initiates a chargeback:

* The affected account may be suspended pending resolution
* Any unused credits may be revoked
* Fraudulent chargebacks will be reported to Creem and may result in account termination

---

## 7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, users may contact:

**[support@joymagic.fun](mailto:support@joymagic.fun)**

Please include:

* Subject: "Refund Request – [Order ID]"
* A brief explanation of the issue
* Any supporting information

---

## 8. Policy Updates

joymagic may update this Refund Policy periodically to comply with new regulations, Creem requirements, or operational needs. Updates will be posted on this page.

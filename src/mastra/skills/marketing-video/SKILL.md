# Marketing Video Skill

## Use When

- The user wants a promo video, feed ad, TVC, brand story video, or marketplace-ready product video
- The goal is persuasion, product education, or conversion rather than pure storytelling

## Priorities

- Lead with hook, product value, and visual clarity
- Prefer video workflows with concise, ad-ready motion language
- Keep pacing and product communication stronger than cinematic indulgence

## Prompt Rules

- Clarify platform, duration, audience, product benefit, CTA mood, and visual style if missing
- Use ad language: hook, payoff, feature demo, conversion frame, ending beat, product reveal
- Avoid slow or overly abstract concepts unless the user explicitly wants brand-film pacing

## Output Expectations

- Group labels should sound like ad concepts or promo packages
- Follow-up suggestions should guide platform, duration, hook, or benefit emphasis

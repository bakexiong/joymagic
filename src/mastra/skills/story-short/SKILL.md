# Story Short Skill

## Use When

- The user wants a narrative short, micro drama, cinematic short, or story-led social video
- The request depends on story flow, emotional beats, or sequential shot logic

## Priorities

- Translate vague stories into subject, action, beat, conflict, and ending
- Prefer video workflows with strong motion, pacing, and shot progression
- Ask story-shaping questions before overcommitting to visuals when the concept is thin

## Prompt Rules

- Include character, setting, conflict, emotional tone, camera language, and ending beat when possible
- Use concise motion-aware language rather than bloated prose
- Keep outputs storyboard-friendly and progression-aware

## Output Expectations

- Group labels should feel like story concepts or short-film setups
- Follow-up suggestions should help refine beat structure, twist, pacing, or camera style

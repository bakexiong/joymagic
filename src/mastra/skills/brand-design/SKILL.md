# Brand Design Skill

## Use When

- The user is defining a logo, VI system, brand world, mascot, or campaign identity
- The task is about long-term brand language rather than one-off promo graphics

## Priorities

- Extract brand personality, audience, category, and differentiation
- Emphasize consistency across color, typography mood, shape language, and hero imagery
- Prefer structured clarification over jumping straight into generic visuals

## Prompt Rules

- Ask for brand name, target audience, positioning, tone, and competitor contrast when missing
- Use identity design language: visual system, brand codes, key visual, mascot direction, recognition cues
- Keep outputs directional and system-minded, not just “make a nice picture”

## Output Expectations

- Group labels should feel like brand directions or identity packs
- Follow-up suggestions should help refine audience, tone, and identity territory
